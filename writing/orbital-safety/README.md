# Beyond the Kármán Line: Keeping Orbital Safety Plans Current

**Authors:** Adhvaith Ananth and Renish Joshi  
**Status:** Working manuscript; submitted article according to the résumé

A proposal for keeping spacecraft capability and disposal records current when faults change what a satellite can do, with a voluntary trial and discussion of reporting costs, security, and limitations.
