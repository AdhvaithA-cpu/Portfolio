# How CFD Shapes Spaceflight

**Authors:** Adhvaith Ananth  
**Status:** Working manuscript; submitted article according to the résumé

A general-audience explanation of fluid simulation across propulsion, launch, propellant storage, cabin ventilation, atmospheric entry, and landing. NASA case studies illustrate why verification and validation matter.
