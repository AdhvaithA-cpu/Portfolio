# Who Pays for the Next Space Station?

**Authors:** Adhvaith Ananth and Renish Joshi  
**Status:** Working manuscript

An analysis of government customers and commercial space-station economics, including public investment, procurement choices, and the limits of hypothetical financing scenarios.
